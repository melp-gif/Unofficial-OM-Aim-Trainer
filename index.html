CONTROLS & HOW TO PLAY

PC

  • WASD — move  ·  Shift — sprint  ·  Space — jump
  • Mouse — aim (click the view to lock the cursor)
  • M1 / Left-click — fire primary attack
  • Q — ability 1  ·  E — ability 2  ·  X — ability 3  ·  R — ability 4
  • C — Tails: cycle laser type  ·  Esc — pause

Mobile

  • Left side — virtual joystick
  • Right side — drag to aim
  • On-screen buttons — fire, each ability, jump, sprint
  • ⚙ button — training panel  ·  everything is touch-reachable

Loop

  • Spawn EXE dummies from the training panel (stationary / moving / attacking / full-movement).
  • Land stuns — every confirmed stun shows the measured duration, impact distance and style points.
  • Watch the 8-second verified stun cap and the post-stun recovery frame.
  • Compare accuracy per character/ability/EXE movement mode in the stats panel.

Training rules

  • No-cooldown is on by default: abilities reuse instantly, but windups, charge-ups and the hammer's 8s return still apply (they are mechanics, not cooldowns).
  • Optional practice variations (instant charge / instant hammer recall) are clearly labelled and off by default.
